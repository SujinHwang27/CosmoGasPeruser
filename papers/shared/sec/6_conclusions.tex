\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

We quantified recipe-vs-recipe distinguishability of the low-$z$
($z\approx\zsnap$) Lyman-$\alpha$ forest flux power spectrum across the four
matched-initial-condition Sherwood feedback recipes, using stacked $P_F(k)$ and a
dedicated binary classifier per pair with three controls. Our conclusions are:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Distinguishability lattice.} At stacking depth $M=64$,
        \latClearedTotal{} of the \latClearedTotal{} recipe pairs not involving
        the stellar-wind/wind${+}$AGN comparison separate above a balanced-accuracy
        floor of $\latQualBar$ on the p16 edge, and the strong-AGN recipe is
        detectable against the other three pooled at $\latCdRest$.
  \item \textbf{Data-volume scaling.} Distinguishability rises monotonically with
        stacking depth for every pair, giving a concrete stacking-depth
        requirement per pair.
  \item \textbf{Controls methodology.} A within-class self-pair null, a
        mean-flux-only baseline, and an injection-recovery sensitivity floor
        ($\delta_\mathrm{min}(M{=}64)=\hfourDeltaMin$) together let a weak signal
        be claimed honestly.
  \item \textbf{Stellar-wind/wind${+}$AGN weak signal.} Previously read as a null,
        this pair is a weak, gate-marginal $P_F(k)$-shape feedback signal that
        emerges only with stacking, reaching $\hbcMtwofivesix$ median balanced
        accuracy at $M=256$ (p16 $\hbcMtwofivesixPsixteen$), nearly orthogonal to
        the strong-AGN template.
\end{itemize}

All results are conditional on fixed cosmology, ultraviolet background, and
thermal history within one Sherwood realization; no nuisance marginalization was
performed. We do not claim per-sightline feedback identification at
$z\approx\zsnap$ from $P_F(k)$ alone. The accuracy floors are operational gates,
not externally calibrated benchmarks. The framework --- a pairwise lattice, its
stacking-depth scaling, and the three controls --- transfers to any matched-IC
feedback suite and to richer observables, and provides the data-volume budget a
survey-scale test of feedback distinguishability would require.
